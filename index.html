FAQ

Good to know.

Who will I be working with?

Me, Ouadie. I have run Fix & Web as an independent developer in Paris since 2021, so you deal directly with the person designing and building your project.

How long does a project take?

Most projects are delivered in 2 to 7 days, depending on the package. You get a clear delivery date before we start.

Can I edit the content myself?

Yes. Sites are built on WordPress, so you can change text, images and products without touching code.

Can you fix my existing website?

Yes. Bugs, slow loading, broken layouts on phones, hacked WordPress sites: send me the link and I will tell you what is wrong for free.

How do payments work?

Half upfront, half on launch, with a proper invoice. Shops can accept cards and wallets via Stripe or PayPal.

Can you make a birthday app as a surprise?

Absolutely. Send me photos and messages, and I deliver a private link or APK ready for the big day.
